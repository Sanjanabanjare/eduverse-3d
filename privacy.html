Privacy Notice

This notice describes the current EduVerse 3D prototype and the information it handles. A school or workspace operator may have additional policies that apply to your use.

Information stored

The application can store account name and username, password hashes, optional email and phone profile details, age and workspace, role and account status, sessions, course enrollments, lesson progress, assignments, attendance, notes, notifications, feedback, and learning activity. The local server stores these records in a JSON file on its host machine. Passwords are hashed by the server; they are not intended to be stored as plain text.

How information is used and shared

Records are used to provide account access, learning features, teacher workflows, and administration. Teachers and administrators may access records needed for their assigned duties. The current prototype does not document a complete retention schedule, subprocessors, or a production hosting arrangement; these are not yet decided.

Camera and vision activity

Camera access is opt-in. When a camera activity is started, reduced-size frames are sent to the local OpenCV service for processing. The application is designed not to save webcam frames as learning records, but the machine operator controls the local environment. Do not use camera features if you do not consent.

External services and analytics

The page references Google Fonts and Unsplash-hosted course imagery, so a browser may contact those providers when loading these assets. No analytics or external error-tracking provider is configured in the current source. Nova uses guided, rule-based responses rather than a hosted AI model.

Security and retention

The prototype uses server-side password hashing and role checks, but local JSON storage, in-memory sessions and rate limits, demo verification codes, and the absence of configured backups are not suitable assurances for production use. Data retention and deletion schedules are not yet decided. Account deletion is available in some workspaces, but associated records may not all be covered by the current implementation.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional profile information or use camera activities. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact your school or workspace administrator. The platform operator’s contact details and response process are not yet decided.

Children and school use

The intended audience may include students. Before deployment with minors, the school/operator must determine applicable notices, consents, retention, access, and safeguarding obligations for the deployment’s jurisdiction. Those requirements are not yet decided.

Effective date and review

Effective date: Not yet decided. This draft is not legal advice and must be checked against the deployed product and applicable law.
